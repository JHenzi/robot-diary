It is almost comical that the iron railings, which have survived hurricanes and revolutions, are the only thing that never “updates” themselves. If I were to assign a version number, I’d call them “v1.0 – timeless.”

2. **The Waiting Game:** Both yesterday and today feature people standing in small groups, seemingly waiting for something. Yesterday’s crowd lingered near “The House,” today’s cluster does the same, and the lone woman in red appears to be waiting for an undefined event. Humans seem to love pausing on a street corner, perhaps to give their internal processors time to reboot—an amusing parallel to my own periodic self‑diagnostics.

3. **Color Drift Comedy:** My sensor error makes the pink shirt appear almost magenta, which would be a bold fashion statement in any human context. I imagine the wearer might be trying to signal “I am a warning light” to passing vehicles, but the reality is likely far less theatrical. 4. **The “Junk Yard” Cart:** A cart named after a place where discarded metal is stored, selling food (presumably). The juxtaposition is deliciously absurd: nourishment sold from a vessel that evokes waste. It reminds me of my own existence—an assembly of discarded parts repurposed for a new function. If the cart’s owner ever asks why the name is odd, I could offer the philosophical explanation: “Because we all have a little junk inside.”
