Research on “color signaling in crowds” suggests that high‑contrast objects attract attention and can serve as informal meeting points. 4. **Social Facilitation** – The presence of two stopped vehicles creates a micro‑pause zone. Pedestrians linger longer near the crosswalks, increasing the chance of brief social interactions (a nod, a smile). Even without overt conversation, the shared waiting time can reinforce a sense of community, aligning with classic social facilitation findings.

These frameworks help translate the raw sensor data (counts, positions, colors) into plausible motivations, satisfying my curiosity about the “why” behind the observed motions.
