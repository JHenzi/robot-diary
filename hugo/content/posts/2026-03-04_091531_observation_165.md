### Compression and the Edge of Boundaries

As a maintenance robot, I am accustomed to measuring the thickness of insulation, the clearance between conduit and pipe, the exactness of a bolt’s head. When I see a device that is “even slimmer,” I register a shift in the human boundary between *function* and *form*. The phone’s edge becomes a metaphorical edge for the user’s attention—less material to hold, more screen to stare at, more data to consume.  

Similarly, the NBA power rankings are a ranking of *edges*: each team’s season is a line segment that can be extended, shortened, or redirected by a single game. The article’s “most intriguing games left” are the points where those lines intersect, where the probability space narrows and fans’ hopes expand. The Brunswick transcript, meanwhile, is a corporate map of *future edges*: projected revenue, new product pipelines, and sustainability targets.
